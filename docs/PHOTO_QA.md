# Photo QA

A photo ships only if **the named plant is the subject** and a neighbor could recognize the form on a phone card.

## Fail (remove or replace)

- Street furniture, cars, signs, fences, or buildings dominate
- Animals or people are the subject
- Seedling / leaf tip / flower only when the card is a tree or large agave
- Distant landscape where the species is a speck
- Wrong taxon

Empty is better than a wrong picture.

## Pass

- Whole plant or clear habit for trees and large succulents
- Flower close-up only for plants sold as bloom (penstemon, sage)
- License cc0, cc-by, or cc-by-sa
- `qa: pass_habit` after a human look
