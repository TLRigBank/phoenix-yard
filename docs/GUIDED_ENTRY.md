# Guided entry and secondary quoting

Specification begins with short guided questions. The house map populates when they are complete. Selecting a wall opens focused per-wall questions for deeper traits. Quoting is a no-pressure secondary offering on the plant sheet only.

## Opening rounds

One question per screen. Large targets. No map yet.

1. Front faces which way? N / S / E / W. Writes `front_bearing`.
2. Who is in the yard? Kids / Dogs / Neither.
3. Water approach? Low / Weekend / Hobby.
4. Remaining brief fields (skippable).

After the last answer the map appears oriented, west wash applied, household flags set. Next line: Tap a wall.

## Per-wall questions

Triggered by selecting a wall. One question per screen. Always skippable with Don’t care.

- Job or piece (Bone / Bloom / Floor, or existing stamp pieces).
- Color mood (Cool / Warm / Neutral / Don’t care).
- Texture (Swords / Soft / Stone / Don’t care).
- Growth pattern (Upright / Spreading / Low / Don’t care).

Answers write into that wall’s brief only. On completion the map updates and the 3-up strip refreshes for that wall.

Deeper traits are available but never required on the first pass. A quiet More about this wall control may appear after the first strip is visible.

## Secondary quoting

Layer A is the intersection of the prior live deck and plants with at least one priced size on the October 2026 Cox Cactus Farm sheet. Cards store dated `ccf_wholesale` with sizes and retail (wholesale × 1.33).

On the plant sheet only, after the existing content, one muted sand-fill control: Sizes · Cox Oct 2026. Hold sentence: Current sizes and prices from the October Cox sheet. Optional.

Tap expands a plain list of available sizes and retail prices. No default selection. Collapse on sheet close.

Multi-wall total appears only after more than one wall is stamped and at least one sheet is opened: muted footer All walls · $XX. Tap opens a plain list (plant, wall, size, price). No cart. No checkout language.

Language: never Buy, Order, Add, Special, Limited, Only, Now, Save, Deal. Always Sizes, Cox Oct 2026, Optional, Current. Prices are dated.

The user can complete the entire walk without seeing a price. The offering is secondary in both placement and tone.
